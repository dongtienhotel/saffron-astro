Kundli

Build your kundli step by step.

A short setup asks for your name, gender, date, time and place of birth, plus your relationship status and occupation. Not sure of your birth time? Tick "I don't know my exact birth time" and move on.

No email or password: the app gives you a private recovery code instead, and no account to manage.

● One question per screen, skip what you don't know
AI Astrologer

Chat with an AI astrologer about your day.

Pick an astrologer persona from the Explore More Experts cards and ask about love, career or health. Replies come from our AI service, with one-tap follow-ups.

Follow-up chips like "Tell me more" and "What can I do next?" keep the conversation going without retyping context.

● Needs an internet connection to reach the AI service
Rashifal

Your daily rashifal, one card per area of life.

Love Life and Family, Health, Career and Money, and Emotions each get a large card. Swipe sideways through them, and tap a suggested question to go deeper.

● Entertainment purposes only - not professional advice
Tarot

FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. Saffron Astro has no email or password sign-up - you get an anonymous recovery code instead, and your birth details stay on your device.

Does the AI chat need internet?

Yes. AI astrologer chat, daily rashifal and tarot readings are generated by a server and need an active internet connection.

Are the readings real astrological advice?

No - Saffron Astro is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

What languages does it support?

English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.

Is Saffron Astro free?

Yes, free to download and use, supported by ads.
